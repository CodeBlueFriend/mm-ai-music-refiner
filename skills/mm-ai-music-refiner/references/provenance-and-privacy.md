# Provenance and privacy

Default to local execution and do not upload audio. Preserve the source hash and any readable credentials. Editing may invalidate an existing cryptographic binding; state that honestly. Absence of readable credentials does not mean absence of a watermark or prove non-AI origin.

Do not include full private paths, audio content, or full lyrics in telemetry. Cloud processing requires separate, explicit authorization with upload scope, retention, and deletion terms.
