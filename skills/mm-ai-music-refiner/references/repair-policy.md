# Repair policy

- A: safe automatic — DC offset, boundaries, isolated click, export peak protection.
- B: automatic with preview — gentle de-ess, dynamic EQ, transient or loudness changes.
- C: confirm before change — pitch, microtiming, formant, stereo width, structural automation.
- D: manual or regenerate — wrong words, severe voice mutation, harmony/content errors.

For every repair, check audibility, goal relevance, source/stem quality, lower-risk alternatives, locality, measurable validation, new-artifact risk, and whether regeneration is safer. A repair lowering another critical metric must be rolled back or marked as a conflict.
