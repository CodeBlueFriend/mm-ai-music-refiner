# Metrics and issues

Keep tuning, key, and vocal range separate. A global cent offset is not a key error. Treat BPM, key, and tuning as estimates with confidence and allow half/double-tempo or ambiguous-key alternatives.

Every issue must include a stable ID, metric ID, category, affected range or whole-file scope, stem, severity, confidence, audibility, evidence, possible causes, repair risk, actions, and status. Do not turn a lossy codec, repeated chorus, high-frequency anomaly, or regular vibrato into proof of AI origin.

Use full metric names such as `microtiming_deviation_ms`; do not invent meanings for external abbreviations such as MD or MP.
